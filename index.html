English Profile

Mahyar Rahimi is an Iranian musician and mixing & mastering engineer working across music production, arrangement, live performance and orchestral direction. His credits include mixing and mastering for a wide range of Iranian artists and projects, as well as arrangement, synthesizer performance, modern controller performance such as Launchpad, live concert work and orchestra leadership.

Selected credits include Ragheb, Reza Yazdani, Sina Sarlak, Rahman, Mahan Bahram Khan, Kamran Tafti, Iman Fallah, Saeed Shariat, Ali Zand Vakili, Mehdi Yaghmaei, Rouzbeh Nematollahi, Parastoo Ahmadi, Mehdi Tafakori, Mostafa Abedini and others.
