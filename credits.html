How these credits are presented

This page does not claim that every online mention is an official industry database. Each entry is tied to an external page where the credit can be checked. Higher-value sources such as Shazam, Apple Music, Qobuz, artist-owned pages and music agencies are prioritized.

Additional credits can be added after independent source verification. Unsupported claims are intentionally excluded.
