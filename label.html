Mahyar Rahimi Label is an independent music label associated with the professional music activities of Iranian music producer, mixing and mastering engineer, arranger, live performer, and orchestra leader Mahyar Rahimi.

About Mahyar Rahimi Label

Mahyar Rahimi Label is an independent music label created to provide a consistent identity for music production, release, and professional audio projects associated with Mahyar Rahimi.

The label focuses on independent music projects and professional audio production, with an emphasis on high-quality mixing, mastering, arrangement, and music production.
