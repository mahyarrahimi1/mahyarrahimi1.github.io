MR Records is an independent record label founded by Mahyar Rahimi. The label is dedicated to music production and the release of independent music projects, with an emphasis on artistic identity and professional audio quality.

About MR Records

MR Records was founded by music producer and audio engineer Mahyar Rahimi as an independent label for music projects and releases.

The label's work brings together music production, arrangement, recording-related projects, mixing, mastering, and the presentation of recorded music. Each release is credited according to the contributions of the artists and professionals involved.
